Why Tech Costs Are Rising Globally Advanced Chips: Manufacturing smaller, faster microprocessors requires multi-billion-dollar fabrication facilities.AI Infrastructure: Massive investments in Artificial Intelligence data centers drive up electricity and hardware demand.Supply Chains: Geopolitical shifts have forced companies to move manufacturing away from low-cost regions.Raw Materials: Growing scarcity of rare earth elements increases the baseline production cost of electronics.Subscription Fatigue: Software companies have largely shifted to recurring SaaS models, steadily raising monthly fees.
